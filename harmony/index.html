Harmony

in the workshop · literature review · specification

Classic color theory says some shapes of colors go together: a pair of opposites, an equilateral triangle, a narrow fan of neighbors. This instrument draws those shapes on a hue wheel and gives back their colors. Then it lets you ask what changes when the wheel changes, and what people actually judge harmonious.

What it will do:

  • Complementary, split complementary, analogous, triad, tetrad, square, hexad, monochromatic or a free polygon, dragged around the wheel.
  • The same scheme on the painter's red–yellow–blue wheel, the additive HSL wheel and the perceptual CIELCh wheel, side by side.
  • The shape carried into the RGB cube, the HSL cylinder and the L*a*b* gamut, where it bends.
  • Itten's contrasts as numbers, the balance to gray, WCAG contrast ratios, and areas for the contrast of extension.
  • Each color on each other color, after Albers and Chevreul.
