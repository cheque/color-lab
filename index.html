Color is space: let’s travel through

Every color is a point in a solid: the HSL cylinder, the perceptual L*a*b* space, the RGB cube. A gradient is a path through it, a palette is a handful of stops along the way, and a harmony is a shape. These instruments draw those shapes and give back the colors.

The math of it

  • Parametric curves HSL cylinder A spring winding through the cylinder: its gradient, the stretch you keep, and a palette spaced evenly to the eye. With the palette-picker widget. widget →
  • Color models sRGB · XYZ · L*a*b* · HSL One color seen in every model at once: the HSL disc and the a*b* plane, the reference white, the sRGB gamut, and a palette you collect as you go.
  • Harmony literature review · specification Complements, triads, analogous fans: the classic schemes as shapes on a hue wheel, and how they change from the painter's wheel to the perceptual one.

Some applications

  • Palettes from a photograph literature review A pyramid of palettes from one image: one color, two, three… each a refinement of the one above.
  • Palette analysis specification Place a few colors in the HSL solid and join them by Béziers that keep lightness, saturation or hue as steady as possible.
  • Random flights specification An arrow flies through color space and shows the disc of colors it cuts. Let it wander, or steer it with the arrow keys.
