Palette analysis

in the workshop · specification

Place a few colours in the HSL solid and join them by chains of Bézier curves that keep lightness, saturation or hue as steady as possible, then resample each curve into a palette.

What it will do:

  • Minimum-variance Béziers for L (exact, one linear solve), S and H (numerical).
  • Cartesian and cylindrical interpolation side by side: across the solid, or around it.
  • Variances, length in ΔE00 and palette evenness for every curve.
